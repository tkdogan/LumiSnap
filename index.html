01 / LUMISNAP DESKTOP

HDR processing and
luminance analysis

Merge bracketed photographs, apply lens corrections, calibrate against measured luminance, and export analysis results.

01

Merge & prepare

Combine bracketed JPEGs into linear HDR with exposure alignment and ghost reduction. Correct fisheye geometry, with vignetting taken automatically from Lensfun, Adobe or your own lens profiles.

02

Calibrate & inspect

Connect image values to spot-meter readings. Explore luminance in cd/m² with false colour, iso-lines, region statistics and a luminance histogram weighted by solid angle. Projects keep their calibration when reopened.

03

Analyse & export

Explore illuminance and glare with Ev, DGP and UGR, compare with evalglare, and export HDR, PNG and CSV results. The glare workflow requires a fisheye lens and a complete 180° hemispheric view.

02 / LUMISNAP FOR IPHONE

HDR capture and
calibration on iPhone

Capture single brackets or HDR time-lapses, inspect false-colour luminance maps and histograms, and transfer saved sessions to LumiSnap Desktop.

  • Adaptive Bayer RAW capture on supported devices
  • HDR time-lapse with an optional scheduled start
  • Frame alignment and ghost reduction
  • False colour with four colour ramps and a luminance histogram
  • Image-point calibration with spot-meter readings
  • Portable session ZIPs and false-colour PNG exports
Read the iPhone workflow ↗

Capture capabilities depend on your iPhone, lens and iOS version. Physical measurement validation is required.
