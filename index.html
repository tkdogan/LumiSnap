01 / LUMISNAP DESKTOP

From photographs
to a clearer picture.

Bring your bracketed JPEGs, Radiance HDR images or iPhone sessions into one workspace for calibration and analysis.

01

Merge & prepare

Combine bracketed JPEGs into linear HDR. Align exposures, correct fisheye geometry and apply vignetting corrections.

02

Calibrate & inspect

Connect image values to spot-meter readings. Explore luminance in cd/m² with false colour, iso-lines and region statistics.

03

Analyse & export

Explore illuminance and glare with Ev, DGP and UGR, compare with evalglare, and export HDR, PNG and CSV results.

02 / LUMISNAP FOR IPHONE

Your field kit.
In your pocket.

Capture exposure brackets, merge HDR and inspect luminance with a native iPhone app.

  • Adaptive Bayer RAW capture on supported devices
  • Saved sessions with false-colour and coverage views
  • Image-point calibration with spot-meter readings
  • Portable session ZIPs for the desktop workflow
