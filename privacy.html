LUMISNAP FOR IPHONE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how LumiSnap for iPhone, provided by the LumiSnap developer, handles information. LumiSnap captures exposure brackets and processes HDR images and luminance measurements on your device.

Information handled on your device

LumiSnap stores the information needed for your capture and analysis workflow locally in its app storage, including:

  • Captured images, including DNG originals and JPEG previews where applicable.
  • Derived HDR images, exposure coverage and reliability data, and processing reports.
  • Session names and capture dates, camera and exposure metadata, calibration points, labels and meter readings you enter.

These files are used to save sessions, process images, display results, apply calibration and prepare exports. They are not automatically sent to the developer. LumiSnap does not require an account and does not operate a developer-hosted storage service for your sessions.

Camera and other permissions

LumiSnap requests camera access to capture image sequences. You can withdraw this permission in iOS Settings at any time; capture will then be unavailable. The app does not request microphone, contacts or location access. Images and session files may contain identifying scene content or metadata, so review them before sharing.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to export or share, LumiSnap prepares HDR files or session archives and opens the iOS share interface. Archives can include original images, capture metadata, calibration readings and processing reports. Files are transferred to destinations you select, such as Files, AirDrop or another app. LumiSnap does not automatically upload these files to the developer or a third-party analytics, advertising or AI service.

The services or people you choose to receive exported files handle those copies under their own practices. If you choose a cloud storage provider, its terms and privacy policy apply.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

LumiSnap does not include advertising or third-party analytics, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell your information. It does not send usage telemetry or session data to the developer. Apple may separately handle App Store, TestFlight or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Saved sessions stay on your device until you delete them. Use the session browser to delete an individual session or all sessions; this removes the session's originals, HDR data and measurements from LumiSnap's local storage. Removing the app through iOS Delete App also removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain data. Temporary export files may remain in the app's temporary storage until cleaned up by the system or the app is deleted.

Exports you saved or shared are separate copies and must be deleted at their destination. Device or cloud backups may retain copies according to your backup settings and provider's retention rules. LumiSnap does not independently synchronize sessions to a developer server. Because the developer does not receive your local session files, the developer cannot retrieve or delete those files remotely.

This website

The LumiSnap website and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Desktop download registration

The desktop download registration form is hosted by Google Forms. It collects your email, name, affiliation, use type and platform. The developer uses these responses to understand adoption and affiliations and report aggregate reach to funders. Registration information is separate from app usage; the form does not measure ongoing usage. Google processes form information under its Privacy Policy. Do not include private registration details in public support issues.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the LumiSnap support issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post private photographs, session files or other sensitive information. Information you voluntarily include in a support request is used to respond to that request and is hosted by GitHub under its privacy policy. You can manage your own contributions through GitHub and contact the developer in the issue about removing developer-controlled copies.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes to the app's data handling will be reflected here and in the applicable App Store privacy information.
